# Erasmus+ KA122-SCH Best Practices & Intelligence

## Overview
Short-term projects for mobility of learners and staff in school education (KA122-SCH) are designed as an accessible entry point to Erasmus+. They do not require complex work packages but focus on **Mobility Activities**.

## Core Objectives (Typical)
1. **Linguistic & Intercultural Competence**: Improving foreign language skills and cultural awareness through direct immersion.
2. **Professional Development**: Enhancing teachers' pedagogical skills, particularly in innovative, student-centred, and inclusive methodologies.
3. **International Cooperation**: Strengthening the European dimension of the school by building networks with partners (e.g., in Finland, Spain).
4. **Inclusion & Diversity**: Ensuring equal access for students with fewer opportunities through transparent selection and support measures.

## Activity Types
- **Short-term learning mobility of pupils**: Typically 10-15 students, 10-30 days, accompanied by teachers. Focus on collaborative learning and workshops.
- **Courses and training**: Staff participating in structured courses abroad (e.g., Finnish education model).
- **Job Shadowing**: Staff observing practices in a host school.

## Budget Categories
- **Organisational Support**: Fixed amount per participant for management/preparation.
- **Individual Support**: Subsistence for participants (per diem).
- **Travel**: Based on distance bands.
- **Course Fees**: Fixed daily rate for training courses.
- **Inclusion Support**: Additional funds for participants with fewer opportunities.

## Success Factors (For Evaluators)
- **Need Analysis**: Clearly link the project to the school's specific needs (e.g., low language scores, lack of international exposure).
- **Impact & Follow-up**: Ensure learning outcomes are integrated into the school's regular curriculum and shared through presentations, website, and staff meetings.
- **Quality Standards**: Strict adherence to Erasmus Quality Standards regarding management, support, and results.
